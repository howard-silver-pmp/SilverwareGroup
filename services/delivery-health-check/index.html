Delivery Health Check

Know what is really slowing delivery—and what to do next

When leaders cannot get a reliable status quickly, the problem is rarely just reporting. Plans, decisions, risks, dependencies, and ownership may no longer form one coherent delivery system.

Silverware Resource Group provides an objective, evidence-based assessment and a practical 30/60/90-day action roadmap.

Why this matters

A busy program can still be difficult to control

The Health Check helps leadership distinguish symptoms from causes before committing to a major recovery, PMO redesign, tool change, or new hire.

You may need a Health Check if...

  • Leadership receives conflicting or late status.
  • Milestones move without a credible integrated explanation.
  • Risks, decisions, and dependencies lack visible ownership.
  • Teams use inconsistent planning and governance practices.
  • Delivery effort is high, but confidence and predictability are low.

What is included

A focused assessment built to produce usable decisions

The standard engagement examines one active program through a deliberately bounded set of evidence and conversations.

1

Active program

A focused assessment of one active program and the delivery system around it.

4

Stakeholder interviews

Up to four scheduled interviews to understand perspectives, decisions, and working realities.

8

Core artifacts

Review of up to eight agreed plans, reports, registers, or other core delivery artifacts.

90

Days of prioritized action

A sequenced 30/60/90-day roadmap supported by an evidence summary and executive findings readout.

What to expect

Clarity without another heavy operating cadence

The standard engagement is completed in approximately three to four calendar weeks, with most analysis performed asynchronously. Meetings are scheduled in advance.

The final deliverables include a delivery-health scorecard and evidence summary, a priority risk and dependency view, a sequenced 30/60/90-day action roadmap, and an executive findings readout.

Clear boundaries

What this is—and is not

This is

  • A focused advisory assessment
  • Evidence-based and decision-oriented
  • A practical action roadmap
  • A bounded fixed-fee engagement

This is not

  • A forensic, financial, legal, compliance, or security audit
  • A generic maturity score or blame exercise
  • Implementation of every recommendation
  • Unlimited access or an automatic retainer
